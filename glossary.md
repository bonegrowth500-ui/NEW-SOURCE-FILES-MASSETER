## A

**Ad Gates** — The three conditions every scaled paid budget waits for: destinations compliant, organic proof, and an offer that converts without ads. *([Module 27](27-the-hub-search-and-paid.md))*

**the Adult Reach Buy** — Paid reach that pushes proven pieces to adults, judged against a holdout and your maximum affordable cost per eligible lead. *([Module 27](27-the-hub-search-and-paid.md))*

**Adults Who Measure** (flagship) — The group of adults who measure instead of guess, where membership is the practice itself: logs kept, captures on schedule, decisions made from the record. *([Module 17](17-identity-and-commitment.md))*

**the affordability question** — The one sentence asked word for word before every paid step, in every channel: "Is this comfortable from your own income or savings, without new credit or buy-now-pay-later?" A no ends money talk and sets the pause route. *([Module 2](02-the-buyer.md))*

**age fork** — The door's legal-adult question ("legal adult where you live"), asked before any field that stores data. A no sends him to the education lane. *([Module 5](05-the-door.md))*

**the Age-Up Dial** — Three packaging choices that lower the minors' share without touching a claim: adult contexts, calm thumbnails, and decision-stage topics. It never aims at an adult age band. *([Module 23](23-long-form-where-trust-compounds.md))*

**the AI rule** — "AI assists behind the scenes, never speaks as you, and clients are told where it helps." You write or approve every review and every reply. *([Module 11](11-the-operating-week.md))*

**the Alliance Check** — The two-question read on the working relationship: does he feel heard, and does the work fit his goal? A dip opens the next review. *([Module 21](21-onboarding-adherence-and-the-plateau.md))*

**the alumni room** — The paid room for graduates, run inside the Hold once about 30 alumni are in it; until then there's no room at all. *([Module 10](10-lifetime-value.md))*

**alumni status** — The dated fact in his record that he reached week 12, the same for every graduate. No exit, purchase, or room can add to it or take it away. *([Module 22](22-renewal-and-referral.md))*

**the Argument Lab** — X as the place to test Canon claims and framings in public argument, captured to email: quote the claim verbatim, argue it against the best objection, log the outcome, and move what holds to long-form. *([Module 25](25-instagram-and-x.md))*

**Ask Everyone, Privately** — A direct, specific, private referral ask of every client at a measured peak, never counted on in the plan. *([Module 22](22-renewal-and-referral.md))*

**the Async Arc** (flagship) — The call's seven beats carried, in order and in the same words, by the result page, a branched walkthrough, sales emails, and voice notes. *([Module 20](20-selling-without-the-call.md))*

## B

**Band Waypoints** — What months 3, 6, and 9 should look like in each reach band. *([Module 28](28-the-first-nine-months.md))*

**baseline day** — The first review-and-decision ritual, in the first days after payment: his first capture to the standard, his first log week, his markers signed with their thresholds, and the signed Expectation Document. *([Module 6](06-the-program.md))*

**the Belief Chain** (flagship) — The six beliefs a purchase completes: Cause, Range, Vehicle, Guide, Self, and Now, with Hold and Share after the sale. Every no names a broken link, though a stop rule can mark one you never work on. *([Module 14](14-the-belief-chain.md))*

**the belief sentence** — The before-and-after belief an asset is built to move, tagged with its link and written before the asset is made. *([Module 18](18-content-that-sells.md))*

**bounded agency** — Genes set the range of what shows, guessing wastes it, and measurement shows what's moving. "What shows" means the changeable column, and measurement never reads a ceiling off his face. *([Module 14](14-the-belief-chain.md))*

**the Branched Walkthrough** — A recorded walkthrough in segments, with his self-assessment result and buyer state picking which segments play. *([Module 20](20-selling-without-the-call.md))*

**the Build Queue** — Your leverage builds, ranked by hours saved each week per hour spent building, taken one at a time as each trigger fires. *([Module 11](11-the-operating-week.md))*

**the Buyer-Mix Shift** — As price nears the ceiling, keep a real tier under it and shift the buyer mix by state through what you publish and how you package it, lowering the minors' share. The adult age mix is read as an observed result, never aimed at through income or age targeting. *([Module 13](13-the-premium-lane-and-the-road-to-50k.md))*

**Buyer States** — The four situations a buyer can arrive in: Struggler, Burned Struggler, Optimizer, and Ambivalent. They are states, not segments, so they decide his route and never the offer. *([Module 2](02-the-buyer.md))*

**buying triggers** — Events that turn a stall he can live with into one he wants ended: a pain spike, failed DIY, a life event, a fresh start, or someone similar succeeding. You respond to them and never manufacture one. *([Module 2](02-the-buyer.md))*

## C

**the Call Cap** — Free fit conversations stay open up to about 6–8 held a week, reserved by tags for uncertain buyers and those near a high-ticket decision. The overflow goes to the paid or async Decision Assessment. *([Module 5](05-the-door.md))*

**the Call Economics Test** — A live call earns its hour when its close-rate lift times the price, per call-hour, beats what that hour earns elsewhere this month. It sets which tags get reserved free calls. *([Module 20](20-selling-without-the-call.md))*

**the Canon** — Your 5–7 calibrated core claims, the Honest Answer first, repeated on a fixed schedule in the same words on every surface. Belief change is a subscription, not an event. *([Module 14](14-the-belief-chain.md))*

**the Canon Lane** — The Canon's email lane: one Canon claim a send, about once a month, to every subscriber except a paused lead. It opens by your first graduation. *([Module 26](26-email-the-private-room.md))*

**the Capacity Ceiling** — The concurrent clients your care hours allow, which caps your new enrollments each month. *([Module 1](01-the-whole-business.md))*

**the Capture Standard** — Matched distance, lens, angle, light, expression, and time of day for every capture, because an honest comparison is one where none of them moved. *([Module 6](06-the-program.md))*

**care minutes** — The minutes of review, group call, and milestones each client takes per week. They set your ceiling, which is why they're protected with rhythm rather than availability. *([Module 1](01-the-whole-business.md))*

**the Case** — One of the Four Stories: one measured client's record, in his words and numbers, placed in your published range with its denominator. It waits for your first range, at 10 graduates. *([Module 16](16-evidence-that-persuades.md))*

**the cash ceiling** — The most a core container may cost the buyer it's built for: about 1–1.25 months of the core buyer's take-home, payable from income or savings without new credit. *([Module 7](07-price-plans-and-promises.md))*

**the Claim Ladder** (flagship) — The rungs a claim can stand on: process → educational → observational → outcome → universal, guaranteed, or medical (never). *([Module 16](16-evidence-that-persuades.md))*

**the claims library** — One document holding every public claim in its exact words, with its Outcome Map column, its Claim Ladder rung, the context it needs, and where it may travel. It governs videos, pages, and emails. *([Module 27](27-the-hub-search-and-paid.md))*

**the Click Contract** — Every hook is a promise, paid in full inside the asset and before any pitch. *([Module 18](18-content-that-sells.md))*

**the Clip Context Check** — Every extracted clip is an original claim: put the caveat back or drop the clip, and a point whose caveat needs a minute of its own goes back to long-form. *([Module 24](24-short-form-reach-and-the-hook-lab.md))*

**Close by Contract** (flagship) — The close that agrees its structure first and runs in order to the ending it named: contract, excavate, summary in his words, one recommendation, the price once and silence, the affordability question about his pick, the ask, objections, the Decision Date, day one booked, and a written recap. *([Module 19](19-the-sales-conversation.md))*

**the Collectability Test** — Could a client who did the work actually collect on the guarantee? Every layer is checked against it. *([Module 7](07-price-plans-and-promises.md))*

**the Comment Courtroom** — Your comment section run on published rules: a comment policy anyone can read, the best skeptical comment answered and pinned, and honest disagreement never removed. *([Module 23](23-long-form-where-trust-compounds.md))*

**the Commit Ritual** — Baseline day's clarity step: decide the first change from his record, let him write his reasons for you alone (never quoted back in any exit), and sign the Expectation Document with every exit and its date in view. *([Module 21](21-onboarding-adherence-and-the-plateau.md))*

**Community Options** — The paid-room choices for alumni and members: the alumni room by default, an in-cohort peer space for one start, or, rarely, an open paid membership that passes parity. *([Module 10](10-lifetime-value.md))*

**the Complaint Budget** — Spam complaints kept under 0.1% of delivered mail and never at 0.3%, with promotions going only to engaged segments. *([Module 26](26-email-the-private-room.md))*

**the Constraint Sequence** (flagship) — The binding constraint moves forward through reach → conversations → care minutes, and it can slide back after a lever is pulled. Read it monthly and pull only the lever that answers it. *([Module 1](01-the-whole-business.md))*

**the Context Stack** — Every observational or outcome claim carries who, what else changed, timeline, capture conditions, range, and denominator, and says it was observed, not caused. *([Module 16](16-evidence-that-persuades.md))*

**the Conversation-to-Content Loop** — Objections heard on calls become content a rung earlier in the journey, built from patterns in buyers' words and never from a protective stop. *([Module 18](18-content-that-sells.md))*

**the core buyer** — The employed Struggler, usually a man between 19 and 32, around whom every default in the playbook is built. *([Module 2](02-the-buyer.md))*

**Costly vs Hygiene Signals** (flagship) — Hygiene signals, such as public prices, refund terms, and a privacy page, are required but cheap. Costly signals are the ones a grifter can't afford to send, such as an honored guarantee with its claim rate published and a dated record that includes the men it didn't work for. *([Module 15](15-trust-without-credentials.md))*

**the Credible Middle** — The position between grift hype and "it's all genetics", answering both of the buyer's questions, about bone and about everything else, each at its evidence tier. *([Module 3](03-the-honest-position.md))*

## D

**Dan** — The default protagonist: the composite core buyer, a 24-year-old Struggler who works full-time in logistics. The full composite cast is in the Intro. *([Module 2](02-the-buyer.md))*

**the Dated Record** — The moat: a published pre-commitment plus a dated log of process and outcome metrics, started in month 1. Honesty can be copied; a dated record can't be back-filled. *([Module 3](03-the-honest-position.md))*

**the Decision Assessment** — The door's paid step, $150–250: a written plan worth its fee to a man who never buys, credited toward a program if he joins. It takes the overflow once the Call Cap binds. *([Module 5](05-the-door.md))*

**the Decision Date** — His personal date to decide by, on or before the last day to join of the start he's deciding for: the next start by default, a later one when a burned buyer picks it or a real constraint dates it. *([Module 19](19-the-sales-conversation.md))*

**Decision Points** — Every assessed buyer meets one real decision point within about 2–4 weeks: a start's last day to join, or an announced Price Step. Credit is never a deadline. *([Module 8](08-real-dates.md))*

**the Demand Equation** (flagship) — Profit = eligible adults × conversion × lifetime value × margin, capped by care minutes. Each term is a lever with a planning range, and the range shows which term is weak. *([Module 1](01-the-whole-business.md))*

**Denominator Discipline** — Every rate you steer by is counted per something you own and count yourself, with the denominator in the metric's name, so a platform's change in what it counts can't pass for a change in the business. *([Module 12](12-growth-decisions.md))*

**the De-Scoping Order** — The fixed order of cuts when a week runs past 25 hours, cheapest now first, and the five things never cut: review turnaround, milestones, Hold and Private deliverables, the content minimum, and the Operator Review. *([Module 11](11-the-operating-week.md))*

**the Design Week** (flagship) — Your week designed line by line to about 20 hours for your stage, with every per-person minute and the build line priced: every hour has a line, every line has a size, and the gap to 25 is slack. *([Module 11](11-the-operating-week.md))*

**the Destination Ladder** (flagship) — Climbing questions from his surface goal ("a sharper jaw") to the destination behind it (taken seriously, at ease, knowing), then one grounding question and a hard stop at the top. *([Module 2](02-the-buyer.md))*

**the Destination Rule** — The page a post links to is part of the post: every claim comes from one claims library, every page uses habit and process words, and nothing carries health framing where an ad or a tracker reads. *([Module 27](27-the-hub-search-and-paid.md))*

**the Dignity Check** — Asks one question of any move: does he leave more capable or more defective? *([Intro](README.md))*

**the Dignity Route** — The way of running a sale for an insecurity-led buyer that keeps force off his worth: stakes limited to time, money from here on, and guessing; no reflection of missed social moments; laddering that stops at the destination; and a recommendation that stays firm. *([Module 15](15-trust-without-credentials.md))*

**the DM Handoff** — Unverified contacts go to the door; verified adults may get one recommendation and a checkout link; Private needs a call. *([Module 20](20-selling-without-the-call.md))*

**the Door** (flagship) — The one owned path from first click to one recommendation: self-assessment with an age fork → result → speed to lead → fit conversation or Decision Assessment → Fit Check and the affordability question → one recommendation. *([Module 5](05-the-door.md))*

**door v0** — The first working version of the door, live in week one: the fork, the screening items, the result, a booking page with prices and the affordability question, the pause tag, and two checkouts. *([Module 5](05-the-door.md))*

**the Dual-Purpose Conversation** — The founding months' fit conversation: a disclosed fit-and-research call of about 45 minutes, with its purpose, the price, and one recommendation stated before he books. *([Module 9](09-the-founding-phase.md))*

## E

**the Early Fast Path** — A section-level list of what to apply in months 0–3 while you read in order. *([Intro](README.md))*

**Earned Labels** — Praise a skeptic can check: each label leads with a count from his record and names the practice the count shows. *([Module 17](17-identity-and-commitment.md))*

**education lane** — Public content and an orthodontist referral for anyone not yet a legal adult where he lives, with no email field and nothing stored. *([Module 5](05-the-door.md))*

**the Efficacy Split** — Raise self-efficacy hard; raise belief in the method only as far as the evidence allows. *([Module 14](14-the-belief-chain.md))*

**effort vs direction** — The read that most stalls you see are direction problems, and that measuring is how he'd know which verdict is his. *([Module 14](14-the-belief-chain.md))*

**the Eligibility Rule** — Who may buy: a legal adult where he lives, plus a yes to the affordability question before every paid step. *([Module 2](02-the-buyer.md))*

**eligible adults** — Legal adults who could pay from their own income or savings without new credit. *([Module 1](01-the-whole-business.md))*

**eligible-adult yield** — Eligible leads per unit of reach, by source: the measure adult packaging is judged by. *([Module 1](01-the-whole-business.md))*

**eligible lead → enrollment** — The one conversion rate you steer by, counted per eligible-lead cohort, because only your door decides who counts as eligible. *([Module 12](12-growth-decisions.md))*

**the End of Guessing** (flagship) — The promise you sell and fully control: a straight answer, a plan he can verify, and weekly review, so that from his own record he knows what's moving, what isn't, and what to do next. *([Module 1](01-the-whole-business.md))*

**the Evaluation Query** — The search a skeptic runs on your name before buying ("[Brand] legit", "[Brand] reviews"), which decides whether he trusts what he's seen. *([Module 27](27-the-hub-search-and-paid.md))*

**Every Plateau Renews the Decision** — A plateau re-read with his own data reopens the decision he made when he paid: continue as planned, change one thing, or stop, with your one recommendation. *([Module 21](21-onboarding-adherence-and-the-plateau.md))*

**evidence tier** — How well supported a claim is: established, plausible, or debated. *([Module 16](16-evidence-that-persuades.md))*

**the exit right** — The week-6 layer of the guarantee: a client who did the work and asks to stop gets a pro-rata refund of his undelivered weeks, claimable until 7 days after the week-6 read. *([Module 7](07-price-plans-and-promises.md))*

**the Expectation Document** — The fuller written terms of the work, sent with the Path and Timeline Card before payment and signed on baseline day: time commitment, what can and can't move, plateaus, the terms that decide his verdict, how reviews are made, and privacy. *([Module 6](06-the-program.md))*

## F

**Fantasy to Expectation** — Name the destination vividly, then the obstacle and the plan in the same breath, with one perspective line that places the destination beyond the face. *([Module 14](14-the-belief-chain.md))*

**the $50k configuration** — The steady state that reaches the top of the target range, adding Program Async and the Self-Serve System to the $25k business. *([Module 13](13-the-premium-lane-and-the-road-to-50k.md))*

**the Fill History** — Each start's seat cap and its fill, published once the start closes, thin starts included, with counts and never names. *([Module 8](08-real-dates.md))*

**the Fit Check** — A two-tier, plain-language check for signals that buying could hurt him, run before any paid step. A signal pauses the sale: no same-day payment, expectations in writing, a cooling-off gap, and no plan. *([Module 5](05-the-door.md))*

**the fit conversation** — The door's human step: a free, disclosed first call of 20–30 minutes that ends in one recommendation, free for every eligible adult while reach binds and up to the Call Cap once conversations do. *([Module 5](05-the-door.md))*

**the Five Scarcities** — Reach, trust, minutes, evidence, and efficacy: what runs short in this niche, each with the move that answers it and what that move spends. *([Module 1](01-the-whole-business.md))*

**Flow Before Stock** (flagship) — The result arrives within minutes and stage-matched sequences carry each new lead to one recommendation; only then do you build for the whole list. *([Module 26](26-email-the-private-room.md))*

**the Follow-Up Rule** — A written recap within 24 hours, one check-in on his Decision Date, and one close-the-loop message, then regular email only, and nothing while the pause route holds. *([Module 19](19-the-sales-conversation.md))*

**force vs pressure** — Force is aimed at the situation, and pressure at the person; pressure is never used. *([Intro](README.md))*

**the founding group** — One standing group that every founding client joins from the first, with a new start every month and review at one-to-one depth while it has fewer than about four members. *([Module 9](09-the-founding-phase.md))*

**the founding price** — About $1.2–1.5k for 12 weeks, just under the opening band, with the next price printed beside it. It has a stated end, a seat count or a start date, and it's never extended. *([Module 9](09-the-founding-phase.md))*

**founding Private seats** — One or two premium seats with fixed deliverables, at about $4–6k for 12 weeks, that give the Optimizer a real tier from the first month while minutes are spare. *([Module 9](09-the-founding-phase.md))*

**the Founding Sprint** (flagship) — First conversations without an audience: every held conversation carries one of five source labels (warm network, replies, Permission-First Replies, short-form, long-form and search), and every no-call enrollment is labeled by the source question at its checkout confirmation, with the founding page and speed to lead serving them all. It aims at 3–6 held conversations a week, with a week-3 source check. *([Module 9](09-the-founding-phase.md))*

**the Four Stories** — Origin, the Stall Told Back, the Transition, and the Case, each carried with its context. The first two are yours and run from month 1; the last two are his and wait for his consent. *([Module 16](16-evidence-that-persuades.md))*

**the Free/Paid Line** — Give away the why and the decision framework; sell judgment applied to him. The method's steps are free, and choosing his next step is paid. *([Module 4](04-offer-architecture.md))*

## I

## L

It never covers appearance. *([Module 7](07-price-plans-and-promises.md))*

**lead-age cohorts** — Bands of time since a man first finished your door (0–60, 61–180, and 181–365 days), by which you measure revenue before deciding when silent leads get re-permission. *([Module 26](26-email-the-private-room.md))*

## M

**markers** — Two or three pre-agreed measures from the changeable column, each with a threshold set at baseline and read without photos, that say whether the work moved anything. Never adherence, never appearance change. *([Module 6](06-the-program.md))*

**maximum affordable cost per eligible lead** — The most you'd pay for one eligible lead: at most about a third of revenue per eligible lead, roughly $10–70. *([Module 1](01-the-whole-business.md))*

**measured momentum** — Round Two's qualifier, read at the week-12 re-assessment: adherence at or above his threshold, and at least one marker still improving across its last two readings. *([Module 10](10-lifetime-value.md))*

**measured peak** — A measurement moment where adherence is at or above his threshold and at least one marker has reached its threshold, so no refund decision is open. Every Measured-Peak Ask waits for one. *([Module 22](22-renewal-and-referral.md))*

**Measured-Peak Asks** (flagship) — Renewal, referral, testimonial, and upgrade asks are made only at a measurement moment that shows progress on his record, never at a plateau, a flat read, or an exit. *([Module 22](22-renewal-and-referral.md))*

**the Measurement Calendar** — Behavior logged daily and reviewed weekly; appearance captured to one standard at baseline, around week 6, and at week 12, then quarterly, and never in between. *([Module 6](06-the-program.md))*

**measurement rituals** — The review-and-decision moments at baseline, the re-captures, and graduation, each ending in a choice he makes from his record. *([Module 17](17-identity-and-commitment.md))*
