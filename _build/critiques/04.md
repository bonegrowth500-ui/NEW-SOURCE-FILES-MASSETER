# Critique — 04 · Offer Architecture
Verdict: needs targeted rebuild · Words: 7,374 (band 6,300–7,700)

## The three weakest sections (rebuild these first)

1. **§5 The Starter Path and the Self-Serve System.** The way back in is fully built, but the free path itself is thin:
   - Weeks 2–3 and 5–7 say only "keep logging".
   - The week-5 change has no free reading rule, though the Free/Paid Line calls the decision framework free.
   - The capture is never used on the path, so it serves only the paid week zero.
   - Nothing follows week 8, and the net is scored by returns alone.

   **Rebuild:**
   - A category-level reading rule: [N] weeks at [log threshold] before judging a change, and logging before levers.
   - A use for the capture that he owns: a comparison at [week 12], skipped if he took one while waiting (Module 17).
   - A loop after week 8.
   - Routes by reason: can't afford, not his money, not now, not a fit, referral, signal.
   - For the Starter tool: its job, the reason for its price, and its trigger.
   - For the Self-Serve System: one job and one page.
   - One number that isn't conversion: week-8 self-checks completed.

2. **§3 One Flagship, Two Buffers, One Net.** It promises "exactly one rung built for each" place, yet slots hold one to three rungs and the unit is never defined (the opening counts "four rungs"). The Hold's job is the slot's, Round Two has none, and the Self-Serve System serves both net and graduates, so "one rung, one job" fails on the module's own ladder. Sam repeats Module 2 and decides nothing; L83–85 duplicate §6.

   **Rebuild:**
   - Define slot (a buyer condition), rung (its own page and one job sentence) and tier (the same rung at another dose).
   - Give the test that places a new offer as a tier, a rung, or a no.
   - Give Round Two a job of its own.
   - Recap Sam in a clause and run a case where the shape chooses: at week 12, Round Two, the Hold, or a clean finish.
   - Cut L83–85 to a sentence.

3. **§2, the Free/Paid Line.** Its test ("does answering it need his record?") breaks in three places:
   - It makes free the Capture Standard, logs and prompts that the Starter tool and the Self-Serve System sell.
   - It would publish the stall rules §5 keeps private.
   - It can't explain refusing the "routine as a PDF".

   §2's own tier rule reads the Self-Serve "upgrade" as gated content. The judgment claim also rests on rhythm evidence, which §5 uses to say contact helps "even when it carries no advice".

   **Rebuild:**
   - Four categories:
     - information: free, in full;
     - judgment on his record: paid;
     - convenience tools: priced, withholding no step or rule, and saying so;
     - stall rules: private and applied only in review, with the reason that isn't gating.
   - The method's steps are free; choosing his next step is paid.
   - Run the test on all three cases.
   - Give judgment a niche mechanism (Module 14): confounded captures, logs he can't read, and stall verdicts.
   - Recap the evidence once (Module 6).
