## The three weakest sections (rebuild these first)
1. **§6 The Call Economics Test.** The formula is right, but the section can't yet make a decision, for four reasons.
   - **It contradicts the Call Cap.** Module 5 and LEDGER C reserve free slots for high-intent buyers. §6 gives the ready buyer a voice note instead, while the "ready buyer" result template still offers "talk it through".
   - **Its Growing hurdle uses care revenue (about $800),** at a stage where care minutes don't bind.
   - **Lift can't be known before data.** It needs "30 events each", and "event" is never defined.
   - **Its routing test can withhold a call a buyer asked for.**

   **Rebuild:**
   - Add a table by buyer state (ready, burned, Optimizer, Ambivalent, in-writing, dignity): each state's default route, and when a call clears the bar. Reconcile it with Module 5.
   - Set the cost by stage: at Growing, the next tagged buyer's call plus the content minimum; at Scaling, $1,250–1,400.
   - Define an event as one routed buyer.
   - Test by changing only what the page offers first.
   - Cover negative lift: the man who'd rather type than talk.

2. **Worked Example.** It contradicts its own templates.
   - Theo's first email "names his cause", but the burned variant swaps emails 1 and 2.
   - His email 4 answers "I always quit", but segment 6 gives burned buyers the guarantee.
   - He sets April 2 by voice note, then email 4 sends anyway, and no check-in is shown. Module 19's Follow-Up Rule allows one check-in and one close-the-loop, "then regular email only".
   - "Each needed Cole once" ignores both Day-2 replies.
   - "[1–1.25] hours" for two enrollments sits under LEDGER A4's Growing 1.5–3, with no framing.

   **Rebuild:**
   - Run Theo through the burned variants.
   - State the rule for a Decision Date set by message: the sales emails hold, and one check-in goes out on his date.
   - Count every human touch, and say the hours exclude non-buyers.

3. **§5 The paid session and the checkout's words.**
   - **The date guard is too narrow.** Module 8 requires "never timed to a start"; §5 guards only "a start's date". A [second Tuesday] session lands three days before every last day to join (April 14 vs 17).
   - **The switch trigger points elsewhere.** Voice notes past the selling line call for folding questions into the walkthrough, not for a live hour.
   - **Gaps:** there's no rule for a typed question that reveals a minor or distress, and no fee.
   - **The checkout's order is unreadable.**

   **Rebuild:**
   - Keep the session clear of every start's join window, and state the fee and its credit terms.
   - Make the trigger the table's own condition, applied after folding.
   - Answer revealing questions privately, never aloud.
   - Give the checkout's order: attestation → tiers premium first → his pick, price once → the question → Fit Check → Card → pay button.
