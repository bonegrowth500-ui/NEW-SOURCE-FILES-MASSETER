## The three weakest sections (rebuild these first)
1. **§4, enforcement at checkout.** The gates leak. Item 7 is free text that "you read yourself", yet warm-route checkouts and plan links take payment unattended, so "everything is ruined" gets charged before anyone reads it.    - after the pause conversation you send a single-use, no-plan link at least 72 hours out;
   - item 7 is read before capture (authorize, then charge after your read), or kept off unattended checkouts.

   Also say when a "no" may return.

   Rebuild: make the result's verdict provisional and argue the plan's from his record. Rest what the fee buys on his record, a named stop list, ranked levers, a dated re-check, and the refund. Add a "don't buy" line 6, and remake the sample as a non-purchase plan (LEDGER C's 25–45% assessment → program makes that the typical case).
3. **§1–§2, the door's order.** "Live hours only on adults who passed everything cheaper" is false as built: the hour precedes the money question and tier 2. So §3's credit-only and snoring no-fits and the opening's checking man still reach calls, and nothing says what a call does on hearing a signal, a minor, or "can't afford". Rebuild:
   - put the Program's price on the booking page;
   - move item 6 into the self-assessment;
   - add one sentence: what a call hears writes the same tag as checkout (Module 19 for the words).
