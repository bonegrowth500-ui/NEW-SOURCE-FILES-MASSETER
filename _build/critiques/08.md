## The three weakest sections (rebuild these first)
1. **§4 Decision Points.** The state table breaks the formula line:
   - The Ambivalent point is "the start nearest his own landmark", which can be months out.
   - The Burned row's "Questioning his stake" drops the forward-stake question Module 2 asks Theo.
   - The Decision Date is tied to a point whose last day (the late-entry close) is never stated.
   - The 2–4-week claim appears five times in about 90 words, and the two credit holds have no stated reason.

   *Rebuild:*
   - A point is a dated start with a stated last day to join, or an announced step. The Decision Date falls on or before that day.
   - Ambivalent: offer the next start, then ask "what changes then?" A vague answer keeps the next start. A concrete one dates the decision to the start after it, with the Starter Path in between.
   - Burned: one stake question, taken as given.
   - The credit's one written statement lives in the written plan.
2. **§6 Announcing Dates.** The sends key off a "final week" that isn't final: Module 6 allows joining through week 2 at that start's price, so "[Month] starts Monday" implies a deadline that doesn't hold. Sequences go unruled. The landmark rule contradicts the opening, the worked example, and Module 2.

   *Rebuild:*
   - Put "join through [late-entry close]" on every send and page row.
   - Sequences state the next start as fact at their own cadence, never keyed to a start.
   - Landmarks follow three branches:
     - a near start-from landmark takes the start after it;
     - a far one takes the next first working Monday, with a Decision Date set now;
     - a life event takes the nearest start now (Module 2).
3. **§5 Fill History and Seat Status.** Founding seats (a licensed variant) get one clause. "Informative" has no threshold. An Early history reading [1] of [8] goes unanswered. The hard case is already settled by the rules.

   *Rebuild:*

     - the founding price's "stated end" (Module 1) is a dated step on a start, announced 30+ days out;
     - "Founding seats are full; [Month] opens at $[next] with [n] seats";
     - the calendar template gets a founding row.
   - Set a threshold from his own records.
   - Build a real conflict: May at [3] of [8], the reserve under a month, June's step announced, and the close rate out of range.
