## The three weakest sections (rebuild these first)

1. **§5 Milestones and markers (W1).**
   - **Photo markers.** Rule 1 lists "how he's photographed", which only a photo can show, but rule 5 says "No marker reads a photo". Drop it from rule 1.
   - **Adherence in disguise.** Rule 6 ("something he can clearly move… by doing the work"), a yes/no presentation marker, and "two of three" make "moved" near-certain for any adherent client, so the clause rarely pays. Test each marker: could a client who logged every day still miss it? Rule 6 becomes: realistic for most adherent clients, guaranteed for none.
   - **Mechanics.** Add who measures, when, and where the result is recorded (posture by spot-check, never the side capture). Add how to pick a threshold: it clears measurement noise and is reachable in 12 weeks. Also add what "moving" means at week 6 and "moved" for two markers (D allows 2–3). A week-6 lever change never edits the sheet.
   - **Hard case.** Add signs separating "misdirected" from "doesn't move". Branch 3 still runs "the adjusted plan".
   - **Week 12.** Add a table:
     - moved and holding → "you don't need it", then the Hold;
     - moving but short (momentum defined on the sheet) → Round Two;
     - flat with adherence met → clause, "isn't moving on this plan", referral;
     - adherence missed → no clause, honest verdict (currently missing).

2. **§4 and the Card/Expectation templates (W2).**
   - **Card block 1** omits the live call, the week-6 capture, the graduation handover, and a guarantee pointer.
   - **"Likely total"** is a formula with an open term ("months in the Hold"). It shows ledger ranges instead of his prices, and it omits the full-intensity Round Two "priced as a Program" (LEDGER B). That breaks "every later offer is an option he already knew about". Fill in the prices, set a 12-month horizon, and show a worked total, plan premium included.
   - **The share** needs a denominator and the mix: offered Round Two, told "you don't need it", clause paid, below adherence.
   - **The Expectation Document** needs:
     - the disclosure fixed (F11);
     - the adherence condition printed before payment (only markers wait for baseline day);
     - "moved" and the turnaround remedy added;
     - "we never score, draw on, or read structure from your face" (F15).

3. **Worked Example, weeks 6–12 (W3).**
   - "[Two] pass partly" is undefined. If it means one of three, the clause owes them; if two, they moved, like Dan.
   - "Of the other eight" accounts for seven.
   - Round Two and the Hold sell without the affordability question.
   - Theo's week-6 read adjusts "by elimination", with no direction check and no written "the miss was yours".
   - Theo's week-12 "the lever doesn't move for him" rests on six weeks of a new lever, with no referral.
   - "The markers were written before anyone paid" is false.
   - Add a client below the adherence condition: the opening has [8] of 10 logging.
