## The three weakest sections (rebuild these first)

1. **§4 The Leak Trace: it doesn't reliably find the biggest drop.**
   - Its ranking rule, "furthest below in proportion", treats every joint as a profit multiplier. But Round Two and the Hold are additive slices of LTV (Round Two ≈ $1.5k of $31.5k at $25k, LEDGER A5). A 30% Round Two shortfall costs about 1.5% of revenue, yet it ranks level with a 30% lead → held shortfall.
   - The refund joint leaks upward, and two joints have no range.
   - It traces the call path only. There is no Decision Assessment branch (2–7% → 25–45%) and no warm-page branch (1–3%). Yet Cole's "about half without a call" means ~95 × 7% × 31% ≈ 2 of his ~5 enrollments go through the traced joints. After a Call Cap, lead → held is low by design.
   - Only one of Cole's joints is below range, so the ranking never runs.

   **Rebuild:** price each shortfall in revenue per eligible lead. Above enrollment, weight it by the share of enrollments passing that joint; below, by that layer's share of LTV. Add both branches, and run a case with two joints below range.

2. **§2 Small Numbers Lie and Denominator Discipline.**
   - Defining an event as the denominator unit breaks the conversion you steer by. Thirty eligible leads at 1.5–7% should yield only 0.45–2.1 enrollments. A healthy 4% rate reads 0/30, below range, 29% of the time, and about 9% of the time twice running.
   - Cole's 2-of-30 lead → held happens 41% of the time at a true 10%.
   - Windows are undefined: the gate uses a rolling "last 30", while the rule says "two consecutive". Cohorts never close, though credit runs ~90 days. "Consecutive" is missing from the formula line and QR.
   - At Cole's ~8.5 held a month, the step brake needs ~7 months, but steps land every ~2.
   - Denominator Discipline never gets its composite run.

   **Rebuild:**
   - Count by the denominator unit only for rates near 15–85%. Under ~10%, count ~30 of the rarer outcome, or read closed start cohorts.
   - Make windows disjoint.
   - Add a rule for windows longer than the step cadence (needs a ruling with 07).
   - Add a dated-break case, e.g. the door's new age-band item.

3. **§6 isn't genuinely hard.**
   - It sets up "starts filled from the waiting list", then says "the next two starts will be thinner", which the list prevents.
   - It makes packaging "the month's lever" when the Constraint Read says care minutes, and the Scaling set doesn't track the eligible-lead count the rule reads.
   - The hard case hinges on "read by case, never by rate", which first appears here, while §1 frames that same guardrail as a rate ("Counted beside everyone else's").
   - It runs only the easy branch and never says what the fix costs.

   **Rebuild:**
   - Pit [N] starts banked against two windows of falling eligible leads, with a rule: if the trend empties the list before the next quarter, packaging becomes that quarter's move.
   - Plant the by-case rule in §1.
   - Add the branch where refund reasons point at the check itself (a line, fixed that day).
   - Price the plainer note.
