## The three weakest sections (rebuild these first)
1. **§6 What the Back End Costs in Minutes.** Parity isn't applied honestly at $25k.
   - **Round Two.** "$1.5k … 0.35 hours … clears a Program hour" works out to about $990 per care hour. That's under the $1,250–1,400 the section itself cites, which is the 25–34 ceiling ($3.5–3.9k at 14 min), and only about at parity with the $25k Program ($3.1k: about $900–1,190).
   - **The Hold at $50k.** A4's 0.6 h is about 45 quarterly reviews at ~10 min each, so the room's prompts and moderation have no minutes.
   - **Rebuild:** a parity table per configuration (Round Two, Hold, Hold + room) with the arithmetic shown. Allowed review minutes = price × 3 ÷ the Program rate; at $49 and about $1,000/h, that's about 9.
2. **§4 Community Options.**
   - The brief's moderation budget appears only as a principle.
   - The open paid membership has no buyer, deliverable, or reason to pick it.
   - The Room Rules have no consequence for breaking a rule in a *paid* room.
   - **Rebuild:** a room line in minutes a week (prompt, fixed moderation passes, safety replies) checked inside the Hold's parity. Define the open membership or cut it to one line. Add enforcement and removal-refund terms, and say whether existing Hold members opt into the room.
3. **§2 Round Two.**
   - "Still climbing" is never made a test.
   - Full intensity qualifies on "the corrected lever is now moving," which contradicts "nothing else does." The worked example's corrected-late client is then told he doesn't need Round Two.
   - The count of corrective weeks is unstated, and nothing bars Round Two after Round Two.
   - **Rebuild:** define momentum as a reading test written at baseline. Full intensity = an outside-plan misdirection, corrected, with the momentum rule met on the corrected lever. Corrective weeks = the weeks the miss cost. One Round Two per sheet.
