1. **§1 What $50k Actually Requires.** "The second tier brings about $14k", but $13.9k of the $24.5k is volume: 13 enrollments against 8–9, so about half again the eligible leads. Credit the crossing with its real value (the core buyer at $2.8k inside the plan cap, about $3k of profit). Add A3's middle case for two tiers (about $14k A, $28k B, $34–35k C–D), so $50k reads as the good-case end.
2. **§2 Buyer-Mix Shift and the crossing card.** The age trigger is an income proxy (25–34 take-home is higher), and the draft never says why that's allowed. **Rebuild:**
   - read the age band only in aggregate, never to route, price, or recommend;
   - decide whether paid audiences may be age-banded;
   - define "working-life contexts" as time pressure, not wealth;
   - specify the G item and start it now;
   - count 25+, since 35+ is ignored;
   - fix M3–M5.
3. **§3 The Parity Rule and its calculator.** It can't yet price a real seat (M6–M9).
