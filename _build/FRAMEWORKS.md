# FRAMEWORKS v2 — register, names, and title (Step 2.3, revised in 2.5)

This is the canonical register of every named framework and defined term. For each one it gives the final name, a one-line definition in the playbook's own words, a **single owning module**, and the modules that recap it. It supersedes REGISTRY.md, which is kept as history only. The Glossary (Step 5.2) is compiled from here and checked against actual use.

**Tiers.**
- **★ Flagship:** the ideas a reader carries for years. Full five-beat treatment (VOICE rule 5), and listed in cheat sheets.
- **◆ Tool:** a named rule, test, or template. Bolded at first use, with a short treatment, and listed in cheat sheets.
- **○ Term:** a defined term, product, deliverable, or metric. Glossed at first use and entered in the Glossary; not listed in cheat sheets.

**Status.**
- **new**
- **reused** (native recap only)
- **extended** (reused and deepened)

## The title

**The End of Guessing**
*The advanced playbook for building a highly profitable facial-development coaching business: offers, persuasion, and the ecosystem.*

The title is the master thesis in four words, and it's true in both directions. For the client, it names the promise the operator controls: from his own measured record, he'll know what's moving, what isn't, and what to do next. For the operator, it names what the playbook delivers: decisions made by thresholds and numbers instead of hope.

## Naming principles

1. **Glass-box.** A buyer who saw the name would find it accurate and unoffensive (the Hostile-Screenshot Test).
2. **Short and concrete.** Two to four words.
3. **No number families.** At most one "Three…", one "Four…", and one "Five…": the Three Ceilings, the Four Stories, the Five Scarcities.
4. **One meaning per family word.** Door, Decision, Check, Rule, Stage, Ladder, Stack, and Record each mean one kind of thing. "Floor" is reserved for the Informed-Client floor.
5. **No clinical or structural words.** No diagnose, diagnostic, protocol, treat, remodel, restore, or reshape.
6. **No source-relative definitions and no aphorisms.**
