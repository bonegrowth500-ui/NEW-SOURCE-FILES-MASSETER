# FRAMEWORKS — the register, names, and title · Step 2.3

The canonical register of every named framework and term: its final name, type, one-line definition, **single owning module**, and where else it appears. This file supersedes REGISTRY.md for names and ownership (REGISTRY stays as history). The Glossary (Step 5.2) is compiled from here and checked against actual use.

**Tiers.** ★ = flagship framework (one or two per module; the ideas a reader should carry for years) · ◆ = named tool, rule, or template · ○ = defined term (glossary only, not a framework).
**Status.** new · reused (native recap only) · extended (reused and deepened).

---

## The title

**The End of Guessing**
*The advanced playbook for building a highly profitable facial-development coaching business: offers, persuasion, and the ecosystem.*

Why: it is the master thesis in four words, and it works in both directions. For the client it names the promise the operator controls: from his own measured record, he'll know what's moving, what isn't, and what to do next. For the operator it names what the playbook delivers: every decision (door, price, launch-free dates, scaling) made by thresholds and numbers instead of hope. It's niche-true without promising anatomy, and it survives the Hostile-Screenshot Test.
Considered and rejected: *Measured* (strong but generic), *The Honest Operator* (sounds like mindset, which is excluded), *Bounded* (defensive), *Signal Over Noise* (generic).

## Naming principles (applied to every entry)

1. **Glass-box:** a buyer who saw the name would find it accurate and unoffensive (Hostile-Screenshot Test).
2. **Short and concrete:** two to four words; a noun the reader can point at, or a rule he can say aloud.
3. **No number families:** at most one "Three…", one "Four…", and one "Five…" name in the whole book (Three Ceilings, Four Stories, Five Scarcities).
4. **No first-word pile-ups:** a word used as a family (Door, Decision, Check, Rule, Stage) must mean the same kind of thing every time.
5. **No clinical or structural words:** never diagnose, treat, protocol (except "Plateau Protocol", which is about the client's program path and passes the test), remodel, restore.
6. **No source-relative names** and no aphorisms posing as frameworks.
