**Sources:** S+ = `S_Tier_YouTube_Mastery.txt` (Part.section); BP = `The_20_Module_Blueprint.md` (M# = module by order). Neither source cites a primary source for any number (BP's one named researcher, Paul Zak, is itself overstated).
**Verdicts:**
- **keep-as-candidate** — plausible convention or heuristic, internally consistent; may enter the ledger as a range with context after a light check.
- **needs-verification** — potentially load-bearing (business, funnel, platform, benchmark) but unsourced, dated, or contradicted; research before use.
- **discard** — invented, fabricated-proof template, excluded topic, arithmetic error, pop science, or superseded.
