# GAPS — Consolidated, prioritized gap map (Step 1.2)

Working file (removed at ship). What the new playbook needs that neither source provides, consolidated from the four slice inventories (`splus-a.md`, `splus-b.md`, `bp-a.md`, `bp-b.md`, §4 in each) and checked against the master `INVENTORY.md`.

**Lens for every gap:** one on-camera operator fronting a named brand, no credentials, 20–25 hrs/week, solo + freelancers, a blank-slate hybrid offer ladder, **no free community**, a profit target of **$25–50k/month**, and the looser Standard Card.
**Priority key:** **P1** = the playbook fails its purpose without it (a business-led, faster-path-to-profit sequel); **P2** = needed for mastery or for the build plan, but a module can stand without full depth; **P3** = light touch or a one-line flag (spec says less focus, or low leverage).
**Coverage key:** *None* = no source material; *Seed* = named or sketched but not developed; *Wrong scale/frame* = present but built for $6k/month, a free community, faceless, or a stricter standard.

## 5. What the sources over-cover (compress or skip in the sequel)

- **Faceless craft and the graduation path** (most of S+ Part 6, plus faceless asides across Parts 1–10). Skip; salvage only the three carriers of authority and "appearance is not the credential".
- **The free-community conversion pathway** (S+ Part 8 as written, and every "that's what the community is for" hand-off). Skip the destination; keep the ask-sizing, service-CTA and threshold-continuity logic.
- **YouTube packaging and retention craft depth** (S+ Parts 4–5 and 10.3; BP M11). Mature, overlapping and high-quality, but the ecosystem is the lightest track. Compress to a refresher plus one master diagnostic.
- **Overlapping diagnostic tables** (S+ has shelf signs, architecture failure signals, packaging autopsy, retention-graph autopsy, feeder signals, metric decoder and a master table). Consolidate into one business-plus-content diagnostic.
- **Claim-trap lists repeated per surface** (S+ packaging, in-video, proof and conversion traps). Consolidate into one claim standard that also covers calls and testimonials.
- **Hook catalogs and copywriting basics** (BP M5's fifteen formulas, power words, AIDA/PAS/BAB, clarity rules, CTA formulas). Too basic for a sequel and several fail the hard lines; keep a handful as named moves.
- **Platform minutiae** (posting times, hashtag counts, algorithm folklore, Reels specs, ESP tiers, tool stacks). Dated and low-leverage.
- **Camera and gear basics** (BP M8 §1–2). One checklist line at most.
- **Audience-research basics** (BP M2 avatar templates, five layers, demographics). Compress into a buyer-state profile with purchase fields.
- **Posting-volume prescriptions** (daily everything across TikTok, Shorts, Instagram, X and Stories). Replace with a time-budgeted portfolio.
- **Pop neuroscience** (hook neuroscience, story neuroscience, "22×", oxytocin trust). Drop.
- **Launches, podcasts and collaborations** (BP M14, M19, parts of M12 and M20). Excluded.
- **Operator mindset and pep talk** (camera anxiety, launch mindset, "keep going"). Excluded; keep only capacity mechanics.
- **Audience-first patience** (S+ long game; BP's months-of-audience-before-revenue arc). Rebalance toward cash-before-audience while content compounds.
