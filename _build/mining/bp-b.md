**Scope:** the slice's ten modules, all read in full. **Reminder for drafting:** the blueprint must never name this source, its module numbers, its title ("Content & Marketing Mastery Program"), or its sibling document. Everything carried forward is re-derived and renamed natively.

**Tag legend:** REUSE = valid as-is · EXTEND = the core is valuable but must be deepened or modified for the new direction · RETIRE = conflicts with the spec or exclusions, or is too basic for an advanced sequel.

**Location key:** M = module (as listed above), § = section in that module, KT = the module's Key Takeaways, L = line numbers in the source file.

**Slice-wide context:** the slice assumes a $6k/month profit goal, two offers ($197 low-ticket program, $2,500 high-ticket coaching), an email-list-centered funnel with no free community, a face-first creator, and launches as the main revenue events.

Evolve it into a per-offer capacity-and-funnel planner for $25–50k/month profit: fix the email arithmetic, use ranges, add margin, capacity and refunds, and show why the numbers force a hybrid ladder.
2. **Six-stage sales call with Clarify the Gap** (M17 §5.3). A sound skeleton whose "What's it costing them to stay stuck?" beat is exactly notch 3. Evolve it into the playbook's signature sales-conversation architecture: belief diagnosis, a prescription moment, price presentation, a direct ask, no-decision follow-up, and a scope/referral-out step.
3. **"What specifically do you need to think about?" plus objection prevention** (M17 §6.2–6.3). The cleanest notch-2 move for surfacing the real objection, paired with the principle that the best handling happens upstream. Evolve it into a niche objection map (adult change, free TikTok info, orthodontist, faked before/afters, "my parents") where every recurring objection gets a content answer.
4. **DM Conversation Framework with a permission transition** (M17 §3.3). A compliant, respectful skeleton ("Would you be open to hearing how I work with people on this?"). Evolve it into text-selling mastery: qualification (age, budget, fit, scope), voice-note use, and DM-to-checkout for lower tiers vs DM-to-call for premium offers.
5. **The application as filter and commitment device** (M17 §5.2). "Effort to apply increases commitment" is notch 7 in action. Add a minors gate, a budget-honesty question, a referral screen, and questions that surface self-stories for the call.
6. **Five buying triggers** (M17 §1.3). Pain amplification, failed DIY, life event, resources, social proof give discovery questions and a content map. Evolve with explicit guardrails: surface and respect triggers, never manufacture or exploit acute distress.
7. **Trust Timeline plus awareness stages** (M17 §1.1–1.2). The seed of an operational "warmth justifies it" test. Evolve into a belief-stage routing rule: never ask for a rung the buyer isn't standing on.
8. **The Real-vs-Fake Urgency Test and five ethical urgency types** (M19 §5.1–5.2). These map exactly onto notch 4 and hard line 3. Evolve them into evergreen, launch-free scarcity mechanics a solo operator genuinely has: slot math, waitlists, cohort calendars, scheduled price increases.
9. **Welcome arc with "The Gap" email** (M13 §3.2). A proven belief sequence, and "information vs transformation" is the central argument for paid guidance in a free-information niche. Evolve it into a named nurture sequence with a direct offer, segment branching, and a no-proof-yet variant.
10. **Automated Sales Machine with twin funnels** (M17 §7.1–7.3). The evergreen low-ticket timeline and the application funnel are the right bones. Evolve them into a full business loop that adds direct-from-content purchase, post-purchase delivery, proof capture and referrals, with enforced-or-omitted evergreen urgency.
11. **Vanity vs business metrics, the funnel-stage metric framework, and the review cadence** (M16). The right instinct to measure toward money. Evolve it into a profit dashboard with post-purchase stages (adherence, outcomes, retention, referrals) and weekly, monthly and quarterly operator reviews.
12. **Content arbitrage and the content pyramid** (M18 §1.2, §3.3; M11 §6.4; M12 §7.3). "Short-form tests ideas, winners become long-form" is high-leverage for 20–25 h/week. Evolve it into a freelancer-run pipeline, and extend the test logic to offers (content winners reveal which problems deserve a paid product).
13. **Conversation-to-content loop** (M18 §4.3). DMs, comments, objections and client wins become content. Evolve it into the research engine that ties the sales and content tracks together, with a consent-and-context testimonial protocol.
14. **Outsourcing order and keep-list, plus SOPs** (M15 §6.1–6.2). "Keep strategy, being on camera, high-stakes client communication, final approval" fits solo + freelancers exactly. Evolve it with claim-check ownership, editor briefs, and an earlier outsourcing trigger based on the value of the operator's hours.
15. **"Followers but no sales" diagnostic** (M20 §7.2). A compact, business-level diagnostic ("Don't hide your offers"). Evolve it into a full diagnostic tree: attention, capture, belief, offer fit, price-to-proof match, ask, conversation skill, capacity and delivery.
