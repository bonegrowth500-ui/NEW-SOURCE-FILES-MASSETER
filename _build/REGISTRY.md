# REGISTRY — Named frameworks & terms (becomes the Glossary)

> **History only (Step 2.5).** `FRAMEWORKS.md` v2 is the canonical register of names, tiers, definitions, and owners, and the Glossary is compiled from it. Names, owners, and module numbers below are pre-R2 and are not to be used in drafting.

Format: **Name** — one-line definition — status (reused / extended / new / superseded) — ⚑ = flagged for Step 2.3 naming review.

Status notes are internal build notes. Glossary entries are rewritten natively in Step 5.2: no source-relative wording, and the STYLE banned-phrase grep applies. Owning modules are assigned in Step 2. Every name must pass glass-box naming (Hostile-Screenshot Test). Terms marked **internal** never appear in the playbook.

## Reused / extended from prior material (populated in 1.2; revised in R1)

### Business & offers
- **Engine over Events** — build a system with inputs, process and outputs instead of one-off content spikes — reused.
- **One Job per Asset** — every asset and funnel stage has exactly one named job and hands off to the next — extended: jobs rebuilt for a business with no free community (content sells; email and the door carry nurture).
- **Respect Without Demand** — content that earns respect but creates no pull toward a paid offer — extended: the core demand-creation problem.
- **Intensity Trap** — authority built on intensity attracts spectators, not buyers — extended: guardrail against over-rotating on force.
- **Big but Broke / Loved but Small** — all-reach content builds tip-collectors; all-belief content builds a tiny room — reused.
- **Frame Ownership** — becoming the reference point that sets the terms of the category's conversation — reused.
- **Positioning Spine** — the worldview the brand stands for and against, which filters every topic and framing — extended: tied to pricing power and a "Name | Brand" link.
- **Neither-Grifter-nor-Doctor Test** — a positioning line should be one neither a hype merchant nor a dismissive clinician would say — reused.
- **Credible Middle** — the underoccupied position between grift hype and "it's all genetics" dismissiveness — extended: pointed tone at ideas and industries only.
- **Assessment-First Spine** — most people don't have a technique problem, they have an assessment problem — extended: non-clinical vocabulary; "baseline, not reading"; sell the decision, not the diagnosis.
- **Struggler Gap Triad** — effort without direction, tactics without feedback, information without interpretation — reused (the offer thesis).
- **Self-Taught Struggler** — the core buyer: months of solo effort, nothing measured, frustrated but hopeful — extended: default protagonist mid-20s (19–28), employed; Burned sub-state.
- **Person-in-a-State** — target a person in a specific state and moment, not a demographic — extended: route by buyer state (see Four Routes).
- **Motivation Segments** — superseded by **Four Routes**.
- **Signature System** — the method packaged as a named, ownable system — extended: process-language names only; packaging in scope, method never taught.
- **Specialist Position** — be the go-to for one narrow situation (the stuck adult self-learner) — extended: tested against price × capacity.
- **Why-Guidance Case** — honest reasons paid help beats free content: feedback you can't give yourself, accountability, belonging, speed — extended: each driver maps to an offer format.
- **Judgment Is the Product** — the buyer pays for judgment applied to him, so offers and prices sell exactly that — extended: offer-design principle.
- **Felt-Familiarity Premium** — people pay more to work with someone they feel they know — extended: pricing lever for a face-fronted brand, bounded by hard line 5.
- **Brand-as-Container** — the named brand holds the method, capture standard and client library; the face carries trust — extended ⚑ (faceless-origin; restate from the on-camera premise or drop).
- **Velocity-Ownership-Fit** — choose each route by whether that traffic most needs speed, an owned relationship, or better fit — reused.
- **Hand-Off Bridge** — the three routes from content to the next step (direct, capture-then-nurture, path-then-ask) — extended: destinations rebuilt around the door and warm checkout.
- **Cash Before Audience** — superseded by **Founding Sprint**.
- **Reverse-Funnel Calculator** — work back from a profit target to enrollments, conversations, eligible leads and reach — extended: reach bands, capacity and margin basis from LEDGER.
- **Client-Results Flywheel** — delivery produces consented, contextualized proof and referrals that feed sales — extended: delivery designed to produce proof; process testimonials from week two.
- **Capacity-True Scarcity** — every seat or deadline claim is derived from a real capacity model or calendar — extended: seat status as plain fact from the public fill record.
- **Real-vs-Fake Urgency Test** — urgency is legitimate only when the price step, start date, credit window, or capacity limit is real and passes the Launch Line — extended ("expiring bonus" struck).
- **Decision Forcing** — without a real decision point, people postpone forever — extended: every assessed buyer meets one real decision point within ~2–4 weeks.
- **Leak-Gate Diagnostic** — trace conversion stage by stage, name who owns each transition, fix the biggest drop — extended: runs past the sale into delivery, renewal and profit; obeys the small-numbers rule.
- **Fix the Leak, Not the Healthy Stage** — find the single biggest drop and fix only that — reused.
- **Master Diagnostic Table** — symptom → cause → fix rows for the whole operation — extended: rebuilt around business symptoms.
- **Stage Lenses** — measure what fits your stage — extended: aligned to the stage definitions and the staged north star (≤6 metrics + guardrails).
- **Operator Review** — one weekly/monthly/quarterly review of content, pipeline, profit, capacity and client outcomes — extended: flags any week below the protected content minimum.
- **Competitor's-Eye Moat Test** — picture a rival three years in: what can't they copy? — reused.
- **Outsourcing Keep-List** — outsource production in order; keep claims approval, selling, review minutes, client communication and public replies — extended: task-billed routing/moderation help from Growing.
- **Floor over Peak** — a consistent, sustainable quality floor beats occasional brilliance — reused ⚑ (aphorism: restate as a quality standard or drop).
- **Systematize the Craft, Never the Thinking** — templates guarantee the floor; judgment reaches the ceiling — reused.

### Belief & persuasion
- **Bounded Agency** — genes set the range of what shows; guessing wastes it; measurement tells you where you are in it ("what shows" = the changeable column of the Outcome Map, never bone) — extended.
- **Grifter Fatigue** — the audience's default skepticism after being burned by fake proof and overclaims — reused.
- **Shame Proximity** — the topic sits inches from self-worth, so name the problem without making the person the problem — reused.
- **Dignity Requirement** — after any interaction, the person should feel more capable and respected, not more defective — reused (operationalized as the Dignity Check).
- **Loaded-Weapon Principle** — knowing where the wound is gives no permission to press it — reused.
- **Jawline-as-Symbol** — the surface goal stands for the destination: being taken seriously, at ease in photos and rooms, knowing instead of hoping — extended: name the desire plainly, never the face as its cause ⚑ (name review).
- **Credible Hope** — lead with hope the audience can believe: clear first, forceful when the stakes are true — extended: restated under the House Standard.
- **Maturity States** — Newcomer, Interested Learner, Self-Taught Struggler, High-Intent Prospect (awareness states for content) — extended: "Skeptical Evaluator" becomes the Skeptic/Burned route in Four Routes.
- **Warmth Ladder** — never ask for a rung the buyer isn't standing on — extended: the test for "warmth justifies it" (warm assets may pitch with public prices).
- **Trust Ladder** — six levels from "heard of this person" to "the right person to help me specifically" — extended: the warmth gauge for direct selling.
- **Persuasion Stack** — attention → interest → belief → desire → action — extended: kept as the content-stage diagnostic, subordinate to the Belief Chain.
- **Purchase Belief Chain** — superseded by **Belief Chain**.
- **Identity Threshold** — committing costs identity as well as money — extended: paid offers cross both thresholds at once; see Adults Who Measure.
- **Social Proof of the Transition** — people like the buyer who changed the same belief — extended: usable from the first founding client (Transition story).
- **Generosity Paradox** — the smarter a serious person gets, the more they value a guide — reused.
- **Education Paradox** — great teaching can teach people they don't need you, so show the real limits of self-assessment — reused.
- **Smarter-Viewer Principle** — give away the why and the decision framework; sell judgment applied to him — extended: the explicit free/paid line.
- **Reframe Finder** — find the one belief to move via a misconception flip, a better question, or a hidden variable — reused.
- **Belief Sentence** — "before this you believe X; after it you believe Y" — reused; tagged with its Belief Chain link.
- **Effort vs Direction** — most stalls are a direction problem, not an effort problem — extended: applied in sales calls and plateau conversations.
- **Four Hidden Variables of a Stall** — demoted to diagnostic categories under **Three Stall Verdicts** (consistency, method fit at category level only, individual factors, timeline).
- **Question Behind the Question** — the literal question sets the topic; the deeper one sets the answer — reused.
- **Retention Spine** — sequence any persuasion asset as the reader's questions in order — reused.
- **Five-Layer Standard** — promise, frame, teaching path, trust, next step — extended: template for pages, offer videos and calls ⚑ (name review; avoid confusion with House Standard).
- **Credibility Moments** — deliberate points where your standards become visible (naming a limit, refusing a claim) — reused.
- **Scope Boundary** — "I can't help with this, and here's who can": confident referral when the issue is clinical — extended: sales disqualification and in-program escalation.
- **Proof Stack** — credibility built in layers, matched to the doubt — extended: across the whole funnel; credential-agnostic.
- **Five Skeptic Doubts** — does this person know it, does it work, are results real, can someone like me benefit, will I be judged — extended: purchase-stage doubts mapped to Belief Chain links.
- **Research Literacy** — openly separating what is established, debated and uncertain — extended: visible evidence tiers + the capture standard; credential-agnostic trust.
- **Process Proof** — "watch me think": reasoning aloud through a case — reused.
- **Skeptic's Piece** — content that teaches people to spot manufactured proof — extended: installs the buying test only an honest operator passes; capture-trick teardowns with faces cropped or blurred.
- **Plateau Analysis** — an honest breakdown of a case that didn't work — extended: feeds the Plateau Protocol and the published non-responder share.
- **Integrity Levels** — three levels of before/after discipline matched to claim strength; the middle level is the house default — extended: tied to the capture standard.
- **Context Stack** — every observational/outcome claim carries who, what else changed, timeline, capture conditions, range, denominator, and "observed, not caused" — extended.
- **Hostile-Screenshot Test** — if you can see the mocking screenshot before you publish, fix it first — reused (also governs naming).
- **Click Contract** — every package or hook is a promise the content must pay off — extended: generalized to an Offer Contract for pages, emails and calls.
- **Curiosity Debt** — clickbait borrows attention against future trust — reused.
- **Proportionality Rule** — intensity never exceeds the payload — reused.
- **Two Hidden Variables of an Ask** — an ask feels like service when it centers the buyer and the tone doesn't break — reused.
- **Intensity Calibrated to Warmth** — soft with cold audiences, direct and unembarrassed with warm ones — reused.
- **Threshold Continuity** — the moment of decision must feel like the same mind as everything before it — extended: pages, checkout, booking, calls.
- **Missing-Belief Method** — "conversion problems are content problems wearing a CTA costume" — extended: find the broken Belief Chain link.
- **Clarify the Gap** — extended into the implication question: he names the stake; you reflect it; future-pace the record, not the face.
- **Six-Stage Sales Call** — superseded by **Close by Contract**.
- **DM Conversation Framework** — acknowledge, understand, give value, identify fit, transition with permission — extended: DMs route unverified contacts to the door; after verification, one recommendation and a cohort checkout link; Private requires a call.
- **Application as Commitment Device** — the effort of applying raises commitment and qualifies the buyer — extended: age fork, affordability question, fit check.
- **Buying Triggers** — pain spikes, failed DIY, life events, new resources, someone similar succeeding — extended: surfaced and respected, never manufactured; fresh-start landmarks.
- **Welcome Arc** — a first-weeks email sequence that moves belief toward the offer — extended: stage-matched; direct offer and no-proof-yet variants.
- **Origin Story** — why the brand exists and what standard it holds — extended: one of the Four Stories; never implies credentials or an undocumented transformation.
- **Objection Prevention** — a repeated objection means content should exist earlier in the journey — reused.

- **Claim Ladder** — process → educational → observational → outcome → universal/guaranteed/medical (never) — extended: placement per the House Standard.
- **Two Failure Directions** — brands fail through intensity without substance or accuracy without attention — extended: the target band is forceful and honest.
- **Maturity Ladder** — six levels from interchangeable content to category-defining trust with a moat — extended: business-outcome grounded.

## New in Step 1.4 (R1) — working names, finalized in Step 2.3

### Organizing models
- **The Five Scarcities** — reach, trust, minutes, evidence, efficacy: what's scarce in this niche and the master move for each — new.
- **Constraint Sequence** — the binding constraint moves reach → conversations → care minutes; choose the door, price gates, call policy and north star for the one that binds this month — new.
- **Belief Chain** — Cause, Range, Vehicle, Guide, Self, Now (then Hold and Share); every no names a broken link — new.
- **Outcome Map** — changeable and measurable / debated / never claimed / during growth; the bone question answered straight — new.
- **The Canon** — 5–7 calibrated core claims repeated across long-form and email; the adult-bone answer is claim #1 — new.
- **The End of Guessing** — the core promise the operator controls: from his own measured record he'll know what's moving, what isn't, and what to do next — new.

### Business
- **Stage Definitions** — Early / Growing / Scaling by trailing revenue plus a volume signal — new.
- **Founding Sprint** — first conversations without an audience: warm network (disclosed), replies to early content, a standing founding-offer page, native short-form, permission-first replies, a personal reply to every adult lead; 3–6 held conversations a week; month-3 gate — new (replaces Cash Before Audience).
- **Rung Activation Schedule** — which offers switch on at months 0–3, 3–6, 6–9 and after — new.
- **Founding 1:1 / Founding Group** — R&D-phase offers priced near the real price with more access instead of a discount; the founding group is sold through the standing door — new.
- **Opening Band / Proof Band** — price bands before and after proof milestones — new.
- **The Door** — the owned self-assessment with the age fork; stage-gated into a **Fit Conversation** (early) or a paid **Decision Assessment** (once calls bind); the **Door Switch** rule — new.
- **Fit Check** — two-tier, plain-language, signal-based check before any paid step; a signal pauses the sale for a human conversation with three outcomes — new.
- **Starter Path** — the humane destination for "not now / not a fit / can't afford": free branch first, a low-cost tool named once — new (renamed from Down-Path).
- **Eligible-Adult Yield** — eligible adult leads per unit of reach, by source — new (renamed from Sellable-Adult Yield).
- **Reach Bands** — A (never breaks out) to D (breakout), with month-3/6/9 waypoints — new.
- **Design Week** — the line-by-line ~20-hour week per stage — new.
- **Protected Content Minimum** — content hours client work can never eat — new.
- **De-Scoping Order** — what gets cut first when a week runs past 25 hours — new.
- **Unpriced-Minute Rule** — every per-person minute is priced, batched, or templated — new.
- **Small Numbers Lie** — decide by thresholds; a ledger range yields to your own ratio after ~30 events — new.
- **Round Two** — the renewal offered at the week-10/12 measurement to clients with measured momentum — new.
- **Hold / Alumni Room** — continuity designed as a paid community of people who keep records — new.
- **Private** — priced to fixed deliverables; month 9+; **Priority Review** as the Optimizer's entry — new.
- **Service Guarantee / Fit Window / Non-Response Clause** — the three-layer guarantee on what the operator controls — new.
- **Collectability Test** — could a client who did the work actually collect? — new.
- **Raise Gate** — gate the announcement, not the raise — new.

### Persuasion
- **Four Routes** — Struggler, Optimizer, Skeptic/Burned, Ambivalent; route by state, not script — new (supersedes Motivation Segments).
- **Burned Struggler** — has bought before and been let down; verification-first route — new.
- **Adults Who Measure Instead of Guess** — the in-group; adult, fit-checked and logging is the status — new.
- **Close by Contract** — upfront contract → excavate → summary in his words → one recommendation → price once + silence → ask → Real-Objection Sort → decision date → day one booked → written recap — new.
- **Real-Objection Sort** — separate the stated objection from the real one and resolve each he raises (up to two probes) — new.
- **Pushback Signal** — the moment he argues for the status quo; the cue to change route (reflect → ask permission → challenge with his own evidence) — new (renamed from Defense Meter).
- **Plain-Language Rule** — action and data vocabulary; therapeutic and medical framing banned, warmth and validation allowed — new (renamed from Control Lexicon).
- **Three Stall Verdicts** — unmeasured / misdirected / the lever doesn't move for this person — new.
- **Plateau Protocol** — pre-announced plateaus, re-plan within 48 h, reward the comeback, week-6 markers — new.
- **Let Him Succeed Before He Pays** — a free 7-day log plus one baseline capture after the age fork — new.
- **Back-Dated Baseline** — the pre-purchase log and capture become week zero of the program — new.
- **Commit Ritual / First-14** — the post-yes ritual and the first-fortnight sequence that answer buyer's remorse with evidence — new.
- **Four Stories** — Origin, Stall Told Back, Transition, Case — new.
- **The Buying Test Only You Pass** — prebunking: teach the tells of manufactured proof and the honest-evidence standard — new.
- **Measured-Peak Asks** — renewal, referral, testimonial and upgrade asks timed to measurement moments — new.
- **Selling-Skill Loop** — recorded, scored calls; an objection log; refunds tracked by promise and path — new.

### Evidence & ecosystem
- **Capture Standard** — the standardized, matched photo-capture rules (replaces "protocol") — new.
- **Measure Behavior Often, Appearance Rarely** — weekly logs; photos at baseline, ~week 6, week 12, then quarterly — new.
- **Observed, Not Caused** — the phrasing rule for published outcome ranges — new.
- **Age-Up Dial** — packaging choices that reduce the minors' share of the audience — new.
- **Verify Page** — the evaluation-query asset: who we are, what we don't claim, what my face does and doesn't prove, policies, prices, guarantee terms — new.
- **Public Fill Record** — seat caps and fill history (including under-filled starts) stated as plain facts — new.
- **Launch Line** — the test separating real dates (offer attributes) from an excluded launch — new.
- **House Standard** — the playbook's statement of its persuasion standard — new.

### Internal only (never in the playbook)
