- **Engine over Events** — build a system with inputs, process and outputs that runs without you, instead of one-off content spikes — status: reused.
- **One Job per Asset** — every asset and funnel stage has exactly one named job and hands off to the next — status: extended: jobs rebuilt for a business with no free community (content may now sell; email and paid entry offers take over nurture).
- **Respect Without Demand** — content that earns respect but creates no pull toward a paid offer — status: extended: reframed as the core demand-creation problem.
- **Intensity Trap** — authority built on intensity attracts spectators, not buyers ("big audience, weak business") — status: extended: working rename; guardrail against over-rotating on force.
- **Big but Broke / Loved but Small** — all-reach content builds tip-collectors; all-belief content builds a tiny room — status: reused.
- **Frame Ownership** — becoming the reference point that sets the terms of the category's conversation — status: reused.
- **Positioning Spine** — the worldview the brand stands for and against, which filters every topic and framing — status: extended: tied to pricing power and a brand-vs-person split.
- **Neither-Grifter-nor-Doctor Test** — a positioning line should be one neither a hype merchant nor a dismissive clinician would say — status: reused.
- **Credible Middle** — the underoccupied position between grift hype and "it's all genetics" dismissiveness — status: extended: sharper enemy tone permitted, aimed at ideas and industries only.
- **Assessment-First Spine** — most people don't have a technique problem, they have an assessment problem, which implies the value of a guide — status: extended: non-clinical vocabulary (assessment, audit, review), never "diagnosis".
- **Struggler Gap Triad** — effort without direction, tactics without feedback, information without interpretation — status: reused (the offer thesis).
- **Self-Taught Struggler** — the core buyer: 6–24 months of trying, frustrated but hopeful — status: extended: buyer economics, objections, offer fit and vulnerability flags added.
- **Person-in-a-State** — target a person in a specific state and moment, not a demographic — status: extended: adds a lower-vulnerability, higher-income buyer as a premium-tier candidate.
- **Motivation Segments** — insecure, optimizer and skeptic-curious buyers need different offers, scripts and safeguards — status: extended.
- **Signature System** — the method packaged as a named, ownable system deliverable at several price points — status: extended: light scope; names never promise structural change.
- **Specialist Position** — be the go-to for one narrow situation (the stuck intermediate) — status: extended: tested against price × capacity.
- **Why-Guidance Case** — honest reasons paid help beats free content: feedback you can't give yourself, accountability, belonging, speed — status: extended: merges the DIY ceiling, "the Gap" and the solo-isn't-enough drivers; each driver maps to an offer format.
- **Judgment Is the Product** — what a buyer pays for is guided judgment, so offers and prices should sell exactly that — status: extended: promoted from proof tactic to offer-design principle.
- **Felt-Familiarity Premium** — people pay more to work with someone they feel they know — status: extended: pricing lever for a face-fronted brand, bounded by the vulnerability hard line.

- **Velocity-Ownership-Fit** — choose each funnel route by whether that traffic most needs speed, an owned relationship or better fit — status: reused.
- **Hand-Off Bridge** — the three routes from content to the next step (direct, capture-then-nurture, path-then-ask) — status: extended: destinations rebuilt; capture is a candidate default.
- **Cash Before Audience** — first clients come from 30–50 genuine conversations, not 10,000 followers — status: extended: rebuilt for a day-1 on-camera brand with no free community.
- **Reverse-Funnel Calculator** — work back from a profit target to closes, calls, applications, leads and reach — status: extended: rebuilt per offer with ranges, capacity and margin.
- **Client-Results Flywheel** — delivery produces consented, contextualized proof and referrals that feed sales — status: extended: delivery designed to produce proof.
- **Capacity-True Scarcity** — every "spots" or deadline claim is derived from a real capacity model or cohort calendar — status: extended: evergreen, no launch machinery.

- **Decision Forcing** — without a real decision point, people postpone forever — status: extended: carried into cohort starts, price schedules and decision dates set on calls.
- **Leak-Gate Diagnostic** — trace conversion stage by stage, name who owns each transition, fix the biggest drop — status: extended: gates run past the sale into delivery, retention and profit.

- **Master Diagnostic Table** — symptom → cause → fix rows for the whole operation — status: extended: rebuilt around business symptoms.
- **Stage Lenses** — measure what fits your stage (retention early, trust signals in the middle, business results at scale) — status: extended: late lens rebuilt around revenue and profit.
- **Operator Review** — one weekly/monthly/quarterly review of content, pipeline, profit, capacity and client outcomes — status: extended: merges content and business reviews.
- **Competitor's-Eye Moat Test** — picture a rival three years in: what can't they copy? — status: reused.
- **Outsourcing Keep-List** — outsource production in order; keep strategy, being on camera, high-stakes client conversations and final claim approval — status: extended: claim approval stays with the operator.
