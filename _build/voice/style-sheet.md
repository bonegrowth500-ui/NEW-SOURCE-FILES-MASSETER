# Style sheet — capitalization and name forms (generated from FRAMEWORKS v2 after the Step 2.5 renames)

Generated from `FRAMEWORKS.md`; if the two ever disagree, FRAMEWORKS wins and this sheet is regenerated. Every drafted module is checked against it (VOICE rule 14).

## Rules

1. **Registered names keep their capitals everywhere.** Words inside a ★ or ◆ name are capitalized as registered, in prose, tables, and headings.
2. **A leading "The" is capitalized only at the start of a sentence, a heading, a table cell, or a cheat-sheet row.** Mid-sentence it's lowercase: "you reach the Call Cap", never "you reach The Call Cap".
3. **Bold only at first use in a module** (VOICE rule 19); never bold a name on later uses or in recaps.
4. **○ terms keep the case they're registered in.** Lowercase terms (the founding group, markers, speed to lead) stay lowercase mid-sentence; capitalized terms (Round Two, Seat Math, the Starter Path) keep their capitals.
5. **Fixed capitalized sets:**
   - Stages: Early, Growing, Scaling.
   - Bands: Band A, Band B, Band C, Band D.
   - Buyer states: Struggler, Burned Struggler, Optimizer, Ambivalent.
   - Belief Chain links: Cause, Range, Vehicle, Guide, Self, Now, Hold, Share (capitalized when naming the link).
   - Build phases: Found, Prove, Grow.
   - Parts: Part I to Part VII.
   - Modules: "Module 5" in prose and cross-references; "(Module 5)" as the pointer.
6. **House Standard items.**
   - The nine things done on purpose are cited in sentence case and italics: *Sell directly*, *Close*, *Name the stakes*, *Use real dates*, *Name the destination boldly*, *Fight ideas, not people*, *Build identity on evidence*, *Present the price*, *Say the true thing*.
   - The six lines (in House Standard order) are cited descriptively in lowercase: "the line on fabricated proof", "the line on structural claims", "the line on fake scarcity", "the line on shame", "the line on vulnerability", "the line on credentials".
   - Never cite them by number.
7. **Offers and products** are capitalized as in VOICE §4:
   - the Program, Program Async, Round Two, the Hold, Private, the Decision Assessment, the Starter Path, the Self-Serve System, the Premium Lane, the Training-Partner Seat;
   - lowercase: the priority tier, the founding group, the alumni room, the written plan.
8. **Brand placeholders:** "[Brand]", and "Name | Brand" with a spaced vertical bar.
9. **Hyphenation and numerals:** week-6 read, week-12 re-assessment, First-14, Month-3 Gate, Two-Win Rule, 12-week Program, door v0, the $50k configuration.
