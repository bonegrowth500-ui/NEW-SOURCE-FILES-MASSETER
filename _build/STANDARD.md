# STANDARD CARD — Persuasion & Claims (calibrated: 1–2 notches looser than S+)

The prior standard was "trust-first": claim hierarchy, dignity requirement, no fake urgency, CTA as next useful step, never pitch the deep offer cold, proof as a stack, survive-the-reveal. This blueprint keeps that spine and loosens it one to two notches — more force, more directness, more commercial conviction — without crossing the hard lines. Every module is audited against this card.

## The governing test (loosened form)
**The Informed-Client Test:** if a buyer later saw exactly what you did and why, and what they got, would they still say "that persuasion served me"? Persuasion that the buyer would endorse in hindsight is allowed even when it is forceful. Persuasion that only works if they never see the mechanism is not.

## NEWLY PERMITTED (the loosened notches)
1. **Direct selling.** Explicit offer pitches in content when the audience's warmth justifies it; direct asks; clear prices stated publicly; selling paid offers straight from content (there is no free-community buffer anymore).
2. **Closing.** In sales conversations: asking for the decision, firmly surfacing the real objection behind "I need to think about it", challenging stories the buyer tells themselves, confident recommendations ("based on what you've told me, this is the right move").
3. **Stakes & cost-of-inaction.** Loss-aversion framing, future-pacing, and "another year of guessing costs you X" — when the stakes are true and specific, and the framing targets the situation, not the person's worth.
4. **Real scarcity & real deadlines.** Genuine capacity limits (a solo operator really has limited slots), cohort start dates, enrollment windows, price increases that actually happen. (Launch campaigns as a topic remain excluded.)
5. **Bolder outcome framing with context.** Speaking to visible outcomes clients have reached, with context attached; testimonial-led content; aspirational framing of the deeper desire (confidence, being taken seriously, liking photos of yourself).
6. **Sharper contrarian & enemy framing — aimed at ideas and industries.** The grift economy, tip-churn content, "it's all genetics" dismissiveness. Tone can be pointed. Steelman before you attack; never mock individuals.
7. **Identity & commitment mechanics.** In-group identity ("people who take this seriously"), micro-commitments, public commitments, consistency, self-selection, social proof of the transition.
8. **Pricing psychology.** Anchoring, tiering/decoys, payment plans, conditional guarantees, value stacking, premium framing.
9. **Emotional directness.** Naming the deeper desire and the real frustration plainly and with force — without converting insecurity into shame.

## STILL FORBIDDEN (hard lines — no notch moves these)
1. **Fabricated or borrowed proof**: fake/paid-for-without-disclosure testimonials, AI-generated or doctored results, misleading before/afters (unmatched angle/lighting presented as evidence), invented numbers.
2. **Universal, guaranteed, structural-certainty, or medical claims**: guaranteeing bone/structural change, promising specific results to everyone, implying coaching replaces dental/medical/orthodontic care, diagnosing from a glance.
3. **Fake scarcity/urgency**: fake timers, invented "spots left", deadlines that don't hold.
4. **Shame & ridicule**: mocking real faces, circling strangers' "flaws", making a person's worth the problem.
5. **Exploiting vulnerability**: pressuring minors; pushing people who genuinely can't afford it after they've said so; exploiting acute distress.
6. **Credential misrepresentation**: implying qualifications the operator does not hold.

## Claim ladder (still used — loosened placement)
Process → Educational → Observational → Outcome → Universal/Guaranteed/Medical.
- Backbone: process + educational.
- Observational & outcome claims: **usable more freely than before**, always with context (who, what else changed, timeline, typical range).
- Universal/guaranteed/medical: **never**.

## Quick audit questions (run per module)
- Would the informed client endorse this in hindsight?
- Is every outcome claim contextualized and every number a real range?
- Is there any shame, fabricated proof, fake scarcity, or medical/structural guarantee? (must be zero)
- Is force aimed at ideas/situations, never at people's worth?

## OPERATIONAL RULES (added in 1.4 — how the hard lines bind in practice)
- **Screen before charge.** Age fork at the door (legal adult where the buyer lives) and a brief appearance-concern screen before any paid step. A screen flag triggers a conversation and a referral route, never a label or a sale.
- **Minors.** No paid offers, DM selling, calls, or payment plans with minors; an education-only lane. A minor's "I need to ask my parents" ends the sale. No parent-facing paid offers. No "younger bodies respond better" framing.
- **Stop rules (force vs pressure).** A clear no ends the ask for that conversation. "I can't afford it" ends money talk: offer the Down-Path once, then stop. Acute distress, fixation, or "everything is ruined" language → no sale, referral. Follow-up is capped and pre-announced.
- **Face-as-proof.** The operator's appearance is never evidence for the method; appearance attacks get a calm redirect to the measurement system.
- **Numbers.** No number in public copy that doesn't come from the operator's own records; templates use bracketed placeholders; every published range names its denominator.
- **Vocabulary.** Assess / audit / review / baseline / decision / plan / habit. Never diagnose, treat, analyze structure, score faces, or use clinical titles. Named refer-out triggers: sleep/snoring signs, jaw pain, bite/orthodontic issues, fixation/distress signals, minors.
- **Surgery/filler.** Never an anchor, never "cheaper than surgery"; alternatives only as a "what each option is and isn't" map with referral.
- **Sub-checks.** Dignity Check (after this, more capable or more defective?) on every emotional framing; Hostile-Screenshot Test before publishing; Payoff Test on every curiosity device.
