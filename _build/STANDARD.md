# STANDARD CARD v2 — internal calibration (never quoted in the playbook)

Internal audit tool for drafting. It is calibrated one to two notches less strict than the source standard, and it records that calibration. **The playbook never refers to the calibration.** The playbook-facing text, written in absolute terms, is `HOUSE_STANDARD.md`. v2 applies DECISIONS R1: a ceiling-and-floor governing test, a Notch Map with defaults and licensed stronger variants, decoys struck, stakes framed for accuracy rather than loss, private-by-default commitments, the Launch Line, and a log of deliberate tightenings.

## 1. Governing test — ceiling and floor

**The Informed-Client Test.**
- **Ceiling:** would a client whose result fell anywhere in the range he was shown, including the low end, still say the persuasion served him after seeing exactly what was done and why? Persuasion that only works if he never sees the mechanism fails.
- **Floor:** letting a screened, able-to-pay, good-fit buyer talk himself out of the right decision fails him too, whether because you wouldn't challenge his story, name the stake, or ask. The floor applies only after the fit check and only while no stop rule has fired. **Stop rules always override the floor.**

Drafting consequence: an ambiguity doesn't default to restraint. It resolves to the notch's default, and the stronger variant is used wherever its license holds.

## 3. Hard lines (no notch moves these)

1. **Fabricated or borrowed proof**: undisclosed paid or invented testimonials; AI-generated or doctored results; unmatched before/afters presented as evidence; invented numbers; the operator's own face as evidence for the method.
2. **Universal, guaranteed, structural, or medical claims**: never claim or imply that habits produce visible adult skeletal change; never promise specific results to everyone; never imply coaching replaces dental, medical, or orthodontic care; never diagnose, from a glance or otherwise.
3. **Fake scarcity or urgency**: fake timers, invented seat counts, deadlines that don't hold.
4. **Shame and ridicule**: mocking real faces, circling strangers' "flaws", making a person's worth the problem.
5. **Exploiting vulnerability**: any selling to minors; pushing people who can't afford it after they've said so; selling into acute distress or fixation; recovery-scam patterns built on sunk spend.
6. **Credential misrepresentation**: implying qualifications the operator doesn't hold.

## 4. Claim ladder

Process → Educational → Observational → Outcome → Universal/Guaranteed/Medical.
- The backbone is process and educational.
- Observational and outcome claims carry the full context stack: who, what else changed, timeline, capture conditions, range, denominator, "observed, not caused".
- Universal, guaranteed, or medical: never.
- The adult-bone answer (Outcome Map) is canon claim #1.

## 5. Force vs pressure — stop rules

- **Force (required where the floor applies):** a stake in his words, a confident recommendation, an explicit ask, resolving each real objection (up to two probes each), a real decision date.
- **Stop rules (override everything):** a clear no ends the ask for that conversation. "I can't afford it" ends money talk: the Starter Path is named once, then stop. Acute distress, fixation, or "everything is ruined" language means no sale and a referral conversation. A minor ends it. Follow-up follows the LEDGER rule: recap within 24 h, one check-in on the decision date, one close-the-loop message.
- **Defensiveness changes the route, not the destination:** reflect his case for the status quo, ask permission to challenge it, then challenge it with his own evidence. Burned buyers are the exception: their "I need to think" is due diligence to support (verification-first route; a decision date he chooses).

## 6. The Launch Line

A date is an offer attribute, and allowed, only when:
- the next start is always purchasable;
- the date comes from the calendar or the capacity math;
- content and email volume don't change as it approaches (one announcement + one reminder per start date, engaged segments only);
- there are no window-specific bonuses, prices, or lead-in sequences;
- no cart "closes" without a next date.

Anything else is a launch, and launches are excluded. No free live enrollment sessions; an optional *paid, credited* group decision session is allowed. Opening prices are an "opening band"; the first group is the "founding group", sold through the standing door.

## 7. Deliberate tightenings (stricter than the 1–2-notch posture, on purpose)

| Tightening | Why it stays strict |
|---|---|
| Fit check before any paid step | Appearance-concern prevalence is far higher among appearance-change seekers; this is hard line 5 applied |
| Minors: education lane only; no email or intake answers from under-18s | Hard line 5; data minimization |
| The operator's face is never proof; no aspirational thumbnails using his own profile | Hard line 1 (an implicit halo is borrowed proof) |
| Public numbers only from the operator's records | Hard line 1 |
| Plain-Language Rule (no diagnose/treat/protocol/clinical titles) | Hard lines 2 and 6 |
| Surgery or filler never an anchor | Hard line 2 (it implies substitution and recruits the riskiest buyer) |
| No countdown widgets even for real dates | Grift-coded for this audience; state real dates plainly |
| No decoys | Informed-Client ceiling: a tier built to be rejected fails in hindsight |
| Sunk spend never a price anchor | Hard line 5 (recovery-scam pattern) |
| Commitments private by default | Evidence: public identity announcements can reduce follow-through; privacy |
| Photos coach-only; pseudonymous, camera-optional cohorts | Privacy is part of the product |
| No free live enrollment sessions | Launch Line (spec exclusion) |

## 8. Sub-checks

- **Dignity Check**: after this framing, does he feel more capable or more defective?
- **Hostile-Screenshot Test**: would this line survive being screenshotted by the category's harshest critic?
- **Payoff Test**: does every curiosity device pay off in the same asset?
- **Collectability Test**: could a burned client who did the work actually collect on the guarantee?
- **Launch Line test**: section 6.
