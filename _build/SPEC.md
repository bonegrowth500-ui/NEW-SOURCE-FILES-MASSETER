# SPEC — 28-Module Advanced Blueprint (locked)

Working file. Removed at ship (Step 5.4). Every phase checks against this. Source of truth = the 56-question interview.

## 1. Purpose & relationship
- Use: the operator's **own playbook** (the operator = the reader, "you").
- Relationship to the two source files: **advanced sequel that also stands alone**. Builds on them, goes deeper, slightly different topic direction.
- The blueprint **never mentions the source files** (no "S+", "Blueprint", "Master Guide", "Module 11", "source file", "the program you read before"). Reused ideas appear as native content, defined inline.
- Recap level: operator's choice delegated → brief, native recaps only where a module genuinely needs a prior idea to stand alone.
- Source conflicts: resolved by a **fresh first-principles call**, not by deferring to either source.
- Terminology: **reuse existing terms where they fit + coin new ones** (named frameworks).

## 2. Direction & themes
- Three themes, **business-led**, ordered **track by track**:
  1. **Business & Offers** (~14 modules): offer design, pricing, operations, growing into a highly profitable solo operation; how to think about the whole business and build it masterfully.
  2. **Belief & Persuasion** (~9 modules): advanced psychology of trust, identity, belief change — true mastery, in service of the profitable solo operation.
  3. **Ecosystem** (~5 modules): platforms and channels; least focus.
  - Plus the **closing build plan** (comes last, built for the **first 9 months**).
  - Exact allocation and phase grouping = open for creation (decide in Step 2). Title = mine to name.

## 3. Business parameters
- Stage: **stage-agnostic**, with **stage notes per module** (operator is currently early; 20–25 hrs/week context).
- Solo model: **solo + freelancers** (project-based help: editing, design). No team-building beyond that.
- Profit target: **$25–50k/month profit**.
- Horizon: mostly agnostic, **leaning multi-year**. The concrete build plan (last) covers the first 9 months.
- Offer ladder: **blank slate**. **Free community is permanently off the table.**
  - Offer types in scope: 1:1 coaching, group coaching, paid community, digital products — **plus new creations**.
  - Profit engine: open, **leaning hybrid**.
  - Front door: open for creation.
  - Style of decision: **options + default pick** (lay out options with reasoning, recommend a default).
- Buyer & audience groups: **open for creation, but not a 180 from the sources** (sources center the Self-Taught Struggler; young men 17–32 core).
- Brand: **a named brand, fronted by the operator on camera from day 1**.
- Operator background: write credential-agnostic (context: operator has **no credentials**).
- Operations (pricing & unit economics, systems & automation, freelancer management, time & energy design): **in scope but NOT in-depth**; other areas get more focus.
- AI tools: **minimal**. Legal & risk: **minimal** (flag only the biggest risks).
- The facial-development **method/techniques themselves: out entirely**. **Packaging** the method into offers/signature system: maybe in scope (light).

## 4. Persuasion parameters
- Applied to: **content & marketing**, **sales conversations**, **client transformation** (adherence, results, retention, referrals). NOT operator mindset.
- Depth: **theory + application** (explain the psychology, then exactly how to use it).
- Standard: **one to two notches less strict than the S+ trust-first standard** → see STANDARD.md.

## 5. Ecosystem parameters
- Platforms: YouTube long-form, short-form video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts), Instagram, X.
- Channels: email list; **paid ads + website & SEO get less focus**.
- Treatment: **refresh then extend**; keep overlap with prior material minimal unless genuinely needed.

## 6. Niche
- **Fully niche-specific** (facial development & bone growth coaching). Principles may transfer, but never deliberately generic/universal.

## 7. Exclusions (hard)
- Faceless strategy · Launch campaigns · Operator mindset/self-help · Podcasts & collabs · Facial-development techniques/protocols · Free community.

## 8. Form
- Length: **~7,000 words per module** (≈196k total). Audit band: 6,300–7,700.
- Template: **Hybrid** — S+-style depth + quick-reference summary/takeaways at the end.
- Frameworks: **as needed**, **named** (memorable names).
- Extras: my choice (see STYLE.md).
- Voice: **Blend** — S+-style reasoning prose + scannable structure.
- Numbers: **ranges with context** (what moves them up/down).
- References: research allowed (psychology research, business thinkers, real businesses) as **inputs**; the blueprint **synthesizes and derives fresh**.
- Modules: **read in order, each self-contained**.
- Success: a clear build plan (last, first 9 months), mastery-level skill, long-term reference, faster path to profit.

## 9. Output & process
- **Markdown**, **one file per module**, plus **Intro & how-to-use** (with table of contents) and **Glossary**. No diagnostic index, no integration map as separate deliverables.
- Saved to **this GitHub repo** (branch `claude/eloquent-hypatia-hrsiaf`), committed and pushed.
- **No approval gates.** User cues each step. Cadence: default one blueprint phase per round (user may change).
- Research depth: Step 1 track sweeps + targeted per-module research.
- Working scaffolding lives in `_build/`, removed at ship.
