# LEDGER v2 — Canonical numbers & ranges

Every number in any module must match this ledger; the ledger is updated here first, with reasoning. In the playbook, numbers appear as **ranges with context** (what moves them up or down), never as single points and never cited. v2 folds in the critique (DECISIONS R1) and the rebuilt scenario model (reach bands, line-by-line hours, capacity from care minutes).

**Status key.** **EV** = evidence-based (tag S/M/W/C) · **PL** = planning assumption (derived or estimated; the operator's own data replaces it after ~30 events) · **RULE** = a policy or design number we set.
**Denominators.** YouTube ratios per 1,000 *engaged* views; email per *delivered*; sales per *eligible-lead cohort*; profit per *operator hour*.
**Margin basis (one definition everywhere).** Profit = cash collected − processing − refunds/disputes − freelancers − software − paid reach. Planning margin is 75–85% without paid reach and 65–80% with it; in the first months fixed costs dominate, so profit is computed from costs, not from a margin.
**Public-number rule.** No ledger PL figure is ever published as the brand's result. Performance, outcome, or client numbers appear in public only from the operator's own records. External research appears only attributed at its evidence tier.

### A2. Stages (trailing 3-month average + a volume signal)

| Stage | Revenue | Volume signal | Binding constraint | North star |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Early** | < ~$8k/month | < ~50 eligible leads/month; < ~15 concurrent clients | Reach, then conversations | Held qualified conversations/week + cash collected |
| **Growing** | ~$8–30k/month | ~50–400 eligible leads/month; ~15–35 concurrent | Conversion + selling minutes | Enrollments/month + eligible lead → enrollment |
| **Scaling** | ~$30k+/month | 400+ eligible leads/month; 35+ concurrent | Care minutes | Profit per operator hour |

A business can be "Growing" on revenue and "Early" on volume (e.g., high price, low reach); the binding constraint decides which playbook applies.

### A3. Reach bands & waypoints (PL, derived; conservative → good assumptions)

Bands are defined by **eligible adult leads per month** (legal adult, passed the door) at month 9. Equivalent engaged long-form views assume 50–70% of raw leads are eligible and 2–5 raw leads per 1,000 engaged views; short-form and Instagram add reach that these view figures don't capture.

| Band | Eligible leads/month (m3 → m6 → m9) | Engaged long-form view equivalent at m9 |
|---|---|---|
| **A — the channel never breaks out** | 10–20 → 20–35 → 25–50 | ~7–50k/month |
| **B — steady** | 15–30 → 40–80 → 60–150 | ~17–150k/month |
| **C — breaks out in year 1** | 20–40 → 60–150 → 150–400 | ~43–400k/month |
| **D — breakout** | 25–50 → 100–250 → 400–700 | ~115–700k/month |

**Waypoints** (per month; new clients means founding 1:1 at m3, founding group at m6, opening-band flagship at m9; cash includes early Phase 2/continuity at m9; month-9 capacity is ~7–10 new clients because review isn't templated yet; hours ≈ 20/week in every band):

| Band | Month 3 | Month 6 | Month 9 | Maturity (proof prices) | Middle case (profit: m9 → maturity at proof → above the band) | $25k floor likely |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 7–16 conversations · 1–3 new clients · cash $1.3–4.5k | 5–13 conv · 1–4 clients · cash $1–7k · profit ~$0–6k | 5–15 conv · 1–6 clients · cash $1.5–15k · profit ~$0–13k | Profit ~$3–23k | ~$4k → ~$10k → ~$14k | Not on organic reach alone: needs one reach lever (adult paid reach, a breakout piece, referrals) plus above-band price; year 2–3 |
| **B** | 8–18 conv · 1–3 clients · $1.3–4.5k | 8–22 conv · 1–8 clients · $1.5–12k · ~$0–10k | 9–35 conv · 2–10 clients · $3–25k · ~$1–22k | ~$6–39k | ~$9k → ~$21k → ~$30k | Year 2 (months ~12–24), once price moves above the proof band or conversion matures |
| **C** | 8–20 conv · 1–3 clients · $1.3–4.5k | 10–35 conv (+0–11 paid assessments) · 2–8 clients · $2–13.5k · ~$0.5–12k | 20–35 conv (+0–28 paid assessments) · 4–10 clients · $6–28k · ~$4–25k | Capacity-bound ~$14–39k | ~$16k → ~$26k → ~$36k | Months ~12–18 (good case by ~month 9) |
| **D** | 9–22 conv · 1–3 clients · $1.3–4.5k | 15–35 conv (+0–18) · 2–8 clients · $3–14k · ~$1–13k | calls capped at ~26–35 (+12–49 paid assessments) · 7–10 clients · $13–31k · ~$10–27k | Capacity-bound ~$14–39k | ~$17k → ~$27k → ~$37k | Months ~9–15; capacity binds early, so price and templated review are the levers |

Readings: **month 3 looks the same in every band** — the Founding Sprint, not reach, decides it. Reach separates the bands from month 6. **Plan on A–B; treat C–D as upside.** Conservative assumptions plateau near ~$6–14k in every band until conversion, price, or capacity moves — which is exactly what the playbook's levers move. $50k is a year-2–3 outcome in Bands C–D through price and leverage, never through more hours. In Band A the spare hours go to reach (more short-form, more personal replies), not to more delivery.
