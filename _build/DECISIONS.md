## Fresh first-principles calls on source conflicts (C1–C40 in mining/CONFLICTS.md)

| # | Conflict | Call | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| D11 | C1 face-era insights | Presence is settled. Keep as day-1 rules: authority rests on judgment and standards, not appearance ("the face is the handshake, not the proof"); structure over charisma; voice + visual system as brand carriers. Retire the faceless strategy and graduation path entirely. | These insights are about where authority lives, which is independent of whether a face is shown; the strategy itself is excluded. |
| D12 | C2 operator's face/results as proof | The operator's appearance is never evidence for the method. Any self-result is one case at matched conditions with the full context stack, never a headline. Appearance attacks get a calm, pre-scripted redirect to the measurement system. | Face-niche + no credentials: using your own face as proof invites comparison, is n-of-1, and becomes the target of every attack. |
| D13 | C3 credentials | Credentials are replaced by visible standards (evidence tiers, capture protocol, refusal/referral policy), demonstrated judgment (process proof), and scope honesty. A scripted "what are your qualifications?" answer states what the operator is and isn't. | Costly signals beat claimed status in a grift-fatigued market; misrepresentation is a hard line. |
| D14 | C4 diagnosis vocabulary | Assessment-first survives as the offer thesis in non-clinical vocabulary (assess, audit, review, baseline, decision, plan). Never diagnose, analyze structure, or score faces. Named refer-out triggers. | Keeps the value (judgment) and drops the liability (clinical implication). |
| D15 | C5 energy register | Baseline: calm conviction. Escalation is licensed by warmth and true stakes, not volume; short-form may run hotter than long-form. "More energy converts" is a hypothesis to test per format. | Trust-fragile audience penalizes hype; force comes from conviction and specificity. |
| D16 | C6 scripting | Hybrid default: load-bearing lines verbatim (hook, core reframe, every claim and number, price, ask, credentials answer, scope lines); the rest bulleted. Sales calls use a talk track with verbatim scope/price/ask lines. | Claim discipline lives in the script; natural delivery lives in the bullets. |
| D17 | C7 center of gravity | The center is the offer-and-conversation system. Long-form is the content spine, email the private room, the owned self-assessment the single door. | With no community, the business (not a channel) is the center; channels are judged by what they deliver to it. |
| D18 | C8 front door | Default: one owned door — free self-assessment (age fork + brief screen; result by email) → paid decision assessment (credited) → one recommendation; warm buyers may enroll directly via a recorded walkthrough or page. Options kept: founding-phase fit conversations, direct application. Rejected as front doors: paid community, low-ticket product (low-ticket survives only as the Down-Path). | Screening, call economics, commodity-proof value, and owned attribution all point to the same door. |
| D19 | C9 email's status | Email capture is the default bridge for almost all traffic (via the door). Email runs flow-first: stage-matched welcome sequences; a lean weekly broadcast; promotions only to recent engaged segments. | Flow out-earns stock; deliverability is a shared commons. |
| D20 | C10 selling from content | Warmth licenses pitches. Warmth signals: returning viewer, subscriber, completed self-assessment, clicked an offer page, replied. | D21 | C11 selling share | No fixed ratios. Warmth-calibrated per surface; one primary ask per asset. Early stage weights more decision-stage and offer-adjacent content because cash matters then. | Ratios ignore warmth; the ask must match the rung. |
| D22 | C12 short-form's role | Short-form asks: follow/subscribe, the related long-form, the door (via profile/pinned). Never a high-ticket ask. | D23 | C13 content mix/build order | Long-form first (the only compounding, clickable surface); short-form as hook lab from week one using clips; offer-adjacent and process-transparency content from day one (it needs no proof). | D24 | C14 one ask | Exactly one primary ask per asset; at most one ranked secondary (subscribe). | Competing asks dilute the paying ask. |
